# Hackathon Brief: Master of One

> **Theme:** Jack of all trades? Master of none. Build the master.

## Overview

General-purpose AI like Instinct and Muse can do a little of everything. But when the task matters, you want a specialist.

In 24 hours, build an AI that does **one useful thing exceptionally well**. Build an agent, fine-tune a small language model, or combine the two. Pick a specific user with a specific problem, go deep, and prove your specialist deserves the job.

## Example Specialists

- A **travel fixer** that rebuilds a canceled itinerary within budget.
- A **recruiter** that finds qualified candidates and backs up every recommendation.
- A **college admissions coach** that turns confusing requirements into a complete application plan.

Choose your niche and define what success looks like.

## The Challenge

1. **Build something new.** No previous cohort or HP projects.
2. **Make it work.** Deliver a working specialist that completes its chosen task.
3. **Prove the advantage.** Test it against a general-purpose AI on the same tasks. Show where it performs better using evals that matter to your user.
4. **Earn the trust.** Face an unseen challenge from the judges. Show how your specialist handles uncertainty, recognizes its limits, and asks for help when needed.

## The Judging Question

> Would you trust this specialist with its chosen task, and what evidence earns that trust?
